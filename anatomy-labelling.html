Anatomy Labelling Hub¶

Every human anatomy quiz set, with the structures still to place. Label opens that set in the shape editor: pick a structure from the list, then Trace and tap (or click) around its outline, using the labelled textbook figure in the corner to find it. Sign in once on each device and every outline saves to your account as you go; the quizzes use it straight away. Structures you have not outlined yet are left out of the quiz.
