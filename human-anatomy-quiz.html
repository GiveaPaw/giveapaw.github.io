Human Anatomy Quiz¶

Test your knowledge of the human skeleton in four modes: multiple choice, find-and-click, typed answers, and matching. Human anatomy is the reference point our animal prosthetic work builds on. The upper limb sets zoom in on the arm and hand, where the wrist and finger bones are large enough to tell apart. Use Customize below the quiz to pick exactly which parts to study and share that quiz as a link.
