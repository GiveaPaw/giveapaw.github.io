Abdominal Organs Anatomy Quiz¶

Test your knowledge of the human abdominal organs in four modes: multiple choice, find-and-click, typed answers, and matching. Twelve textbook illustrations cover the digestive tract from mouth to anus, the stomach and intestines, the liver, gallbladder, pancreas and spleen, the kidneys and bladder, and the muscles of the abdominal wall.
