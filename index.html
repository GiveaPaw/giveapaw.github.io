About the Program

Give a Paw is a UC Berkeley program designing and manufacturing no-cost prosthetics for animals in the Bay Area. Personalized healthcare depends on trained specialists, and there are not enough of them to reach everyone who needs care.

"Only 1 in 10 people in need has access to assistive products, including prostheses and orthoses, because of their high cost and because of lack of awareness, availability, and trained personnel."

— World Health Organization

We work on all three of those obstacles at once: the designs are generated rather than hand-sculpted, the documentation on this site is public, and the program itself trains new people every semester through the Canine Prosthetics DeCal run by Bioprinting@Berkeley. Meet the team behind the work.

Special thanks to Max, Billie, Yana, Ollie, and Kiki for participating in our program.
