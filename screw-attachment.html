Screw Attachment¶

Every socket is fastened to the rest of the prosthetic with screws, and each screw goes through a small round attachment that nTop builds onto the socket. Five numbers in the attachment block decide its shape. Each model below is a real nTop export: 2,498 versions of the attachment, one for every setting on the sliders.

Step through the slides to see what each number does, then try real screw sizes, or set all five yourself in the explorer.

How these were made: the attachment notebook (10-23_attachment.ntop) was run through nTop Automate for every setting on the sliders: each number on its own with the others at 15 / 4 / 2.8 / 7 / 2.8 mm, nine standard screws, and a 2,310-case explorer grid that skips combinations where the head is not wider than the shank or the pad is less than 2 mm wider than the head. Five explorer cases (10 mm pad, Grip 5, Head Diameter 8, Head Height 5) failed to mesh in nTop and are missing. Each export went through the site's usual model pipeline (Draco GLB, about 20 KB each). The part is shown flipped so the screw goes in from the top; in nTop's frame the head end sits at z = 0. The screw is drawn by this page, not exported from nTop, sized to fit the hole exactly.
