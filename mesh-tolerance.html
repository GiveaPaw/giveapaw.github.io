Mesh Tolerance¶

When nTop turns a socket into a mesh, the Tolerance input decides how closely the triangles follow the true shape. A small tolerance gives a big, exact file; a large one gives a small, rough file. The website then compresses every model the same way before showing it: specks under 100 triangles are dropped, the mesh is capped at 150,000 triangles, and it is saved as a Draco-compressed GLB.

Below is Ollie's blended socket with four attachments, exported at 20 tolerances from 0.1 to 3.9 mm (Min Blend 0, Max Blend 60, Connector Blend Radius 5 mm). Each model in the viewer is the compressed file the website would load. Drag the slider to see what each tolerance costs and what it loses.
