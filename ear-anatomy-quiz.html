Ear Anatomy Quiz¶

Test your knowledge of the human ear in four modes: multiple choice, find-and-click, typed answers, and matching. The external ear is the part we scan, model, and print when we work on auricular prostheses, so its landmarks get their own set; a second set walks through the cross-section from the auricle to the cochlea and the vestibulocochlear nerve. Both views are of a right ear. Use Customize below the quiz to pick exactly which parts to study and share that quiz as a link.
