Knee Anatomy Quiz¶

Test your knowledge of the human knee in four modes: multiple choice, find-and-click, typed answers, and matching. The knee is the joint our canine stifle work is most often compared against, so its bones, ligaments, tendons, and muscles are worth knowing cold. Four textbook illustrations cover the joint itself and the muscles of the thigh and calf that act on it; every view is of a right leg.
