Anatomy Learning Lab¶

Five ways of studying anatomy that research says work better than rereading or staring at a labelled diagram. Each demo runs on the outlines traced for the site’s quizzes (the dog and human skeletons and the ear), and every set you finish in the labelling editor joins them automatically. They work with a finger, an Apple Pencil or a mouse.
